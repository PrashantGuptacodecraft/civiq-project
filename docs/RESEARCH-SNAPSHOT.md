# Research Snapshot

**Last checked:** 6 October 2026

CivIQ was designed after comparing the project concept with current Indian civic, emergency, disaster, urban-data, identity, language, and interoperability ecosystems. The design deliberately complements existing systems rather than replacing them.

See `docs/research/REFERENCE-SYSTEMS.md` for the working integration map and source URLs.

Key implications:
- CPGRAMS already covers national grievance routing/tracking and uses AI/ML for repetitive/spam grievance analysis; CivIQ should differentiate through the end-to-end trust, field-proof, asset and cross-domain incident layer.
- 112 is the emergency service handoff; CivIQ should not present itself as an emergency replacement.
- SACHET is an official disaster-alert source; CivIQ should consume/represent authorized alerts and build the ground-situation/operations layer.
- IUDX and LGD point toward standardized urban data and jurisdiction modeling.
- e-Pramaan/MeriPehchaan are references for future authorized government identity/SSO integration.
- BHASHINI is a strong reference for Indian-language and voice capabilities.
- DPDP Rules 2025 make privacy-by-design part of the baseline for a production-oriented Indian system.
