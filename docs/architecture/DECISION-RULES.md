# CivIQ Decision Rules

1. **Identity is not truth.** A verified account can still make an incorrect report.
2. **AI is not authority.** AI outputs are recommendations with confidence and explanations.
3. **Emergency path is fail-safe.** Do not block urgent help while waiting for verification; mark the CivIQ incident unverified and provide the official emergency handoff.
4. **Official sources remain authoritative.** CivIQ must preserve provenance and clearly distinguish imported official alerts from citizen reports and AI inferences.
5. **Evidence is not proof by itself.** EXIF, GPS and image classifiers are signals. Ground verification is stronger.
6. **High-impact actions require human authorization.** Use one- or two-person approval according to configured risk level.
7. **Audit history is append-only.** Corrections create new events; they do not erase old decisions.
8. **National-ready does not mean nationally deployed.** Pilot with synthetic/mock integrations unless an authorized live integration exists.
